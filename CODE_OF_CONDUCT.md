# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume the person on the other end is trying to help, including when they
are telling you something you did is broken.

## What is expected

- **Write bug reports about the software, not about the person who wrote it.** "The cleaner
  deleted my pinned folders" is a good report. Adding a judgement about the competence of
  whoever shipped it adds nothing a fix can use.
- **Take the same care when receiving a report.** Someone who took the time to describe a
  problem is doing unpaid work for this project. A dismissive reply costs the next report too,
  because they read this thread first.
- **Respect that people write in six languages here.** A reply in imperfect English is someone
  meeting you more than halfway. Do not correct the grammar of a bug report.
- **Do not post other people's data.** Not screenshots with their filenames in it, not logs
  with their paths, not anything from a shared machine.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, insults, or demeaning comments about anyone. Publishing someone's private
information. Sustained disruption of discussions. Sexual attention or advances of any kind.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour here is a problem, report it privately through
[Security advisories](../../security/advisories/new) — it is the only private channel this
repository has, and it will be read.

Reports are handled confidentially. Responses range from a request to edit a comment, through
removing content, to blocking an account from this repository, according to how serious and how
repeated the behaviour is.

## Scope

This applies in issues, discussions, pull requests and release comments in this repository, and
to anyone representing the project elsewhere.
