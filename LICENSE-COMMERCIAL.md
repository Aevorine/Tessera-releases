## 1. Why this exists

The community edition is licensed under the GNU Affero General Public License
v3.0. AGPL requires that anyone who receives the software — including anyone
who merely *uses it over a network* — can obtain the complete corresponding
source code, under the AGPL, for the whole work it is part of.

For most users that is not a problem. For some it is a hard blocker:

- The product it would be embedded in is closed-source
- It would be offered as a hosted service, and the surrounding stack cannot be published
- Procurement or legal policy prohibits copyleft dependencies in shipped products

This licence removes that obligation, in exchange for a fee.
